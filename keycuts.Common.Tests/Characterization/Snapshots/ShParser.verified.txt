## drift
Shortcut:    |drift|
Type:        Url
Target:      |https://example.com|
OpenWithApp: |/Applications/Firefox.app|
Command:     |open -a '/Applications/Firefox.app' 'https://example.org'|
Description: |Edited by hand|
Note:        The header's url wins over the Firefox launch line, as the REM lines do in a .bat. Command keeps the real line, and OpenWithApp comes from it.
