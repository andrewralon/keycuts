# Runner.BuildShortcutLines output. <version> stands for Runner.Version.

## url
|@ECHO OFF|
|REM keycuts <version>|
|REM <shortcut>C:\Shortcuts\url.bat</shortcut>|
|REM <type>url</type>|
|REM <target>https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#inbox</target>|
|REM <description></description>|
|START https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#inbox|
|EXIT|
