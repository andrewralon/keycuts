The app icon, bigger — v2 (B, with and without its tile)

Art B: on its tile the key takes 96% of it at 48 px and up (the shadow clipped to the tile) and all of it below. Transparent is the same key with no tile and no shadow (a shadow cut off at the square edge of the image would show), filling its frame. Porcelain’s transparent version is the charcoal key, onyx’s the white key, and cobalt and twilight share the blue key. Each panel is a background the icon can land on: light, dark and a wallpaper. The tab bar is R3: no socket, 36 px.
